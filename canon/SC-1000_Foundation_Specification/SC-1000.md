# 1. Purpose

This Canon establishes the architectural foundation of SmartScreenOS.

It defines the permanent principles upon which every Platform component, Capability, Runtime subsystem, Canon specification and engineering implementation SHALL be constructed.

The objectives of this Canon are to:

- establish the architectural identity of SmartScreenOS;
- define the fundamental concepts governing the Platform;
- provide a stable foundation for future Canon documents;
- ensure architectural consistency across all implementations;
- define the invariant principles that SHALL remain valid throughout the evolution of SmartScreenOS.

This document SHALL be regarded as the constitutional foundation of the SmartScreenOS Platform.

---

# 2. Scope

This Canon applies to every architectural element within SmartScreenOS.

The scope includes:

- Platform Architecture
- Runtime
- Semantic Layer
- Capability Framework
- Household Model
- Dashboard
- Services
- Device Integration
- Engineering Standards
- Canon Specifications

This Canon does not define implementation algorithms, programming language requirements or vendor-specific technologies.

---

# 3. Authority

SC-1000 is a Normative Canon.

All future Canon documents SHALL conform to the principles established herein.

When architectural conflicts occur, the requirements defined by SC-1000 SHALL take precedence unless superseded by an officially approved future version of this Canon.

Implementations SHALL NOT redefine any foundational principle established by this document.

---

# 4. Foundation Principles

The following principles constitute the permanent architectural foundation of SmartScreenOS.

---

## FP-001 Platform First

SmartScreenOS SHALL be designed as a Platform rather than as an application.

Applications, dashboards and automation logic SHALL exist as consumers of Platform capabilities.

The Platform SHALL remain independent from any single application.

---

## FP-002 Household First

The Household SHALL be the primary semantic object of SmartScreenOS.

Devices, Rooms and Residents SHALL exist as elements of the Household rather than as isolated entities.

All semantic understanding SHALL ultimately describe the Household.

---

## FP-003 Observation Before Interpretation

Observed facts SHALL remain distinct from interpreted meaning.

Raw observations SHALL never contain assumptions, predictions or semantic conclusions.

Interpretation SHALL occur only after sufficient Context has been established.

---

## FP-004 Semantic Before Automation

The Platform SHALL first understand the Household before attempting to influence it.

Automation without semantic understanding SHALL be considered incomplete.

Recommendations SHALL be preferred over blind execution whenever uncertainty exists.

---

## FP-005 Experience Driven

The ultimate objective of SmartScreenOS SHALL be improvement of Household Experience.

Device control, automation and optimization SHALL exist only as means to improve Experience.

No subsystem SHALL optimize hardware behavior at the expense of Household Experience.

---

## FP-006 Explainability

Every significant Decision generated by the Platform SHOULD be explainable.

The Platform SHALL preserve sufficient traceability to reconstruct the reasoning process leading to a Decision.

Opaque architectural behavior SHALL be avoided whenever practical.

# 5. Platform Philosophy

SmartScreenOS is a Household Cognitive Platform.

Its purpose is to understand the Household, improve Household Experience and provide explainable assistance to Residents.

The Platform SHALL remain independent of implementation technologies, hardware vendors and automation ecosystems.

---

# 5.1 Platform Goals

The following goals define the permanent objectives of SmartScreenOS.

## PG-001 Understand

The Platform SHALL continuously improve its understanding of the Household.

Understanding SHALL precede automation.

---

## PG-002 Assist

The Platform SHALL assist Residents rather than replace their decisions.

Automation exists to support human life rather than control it.

---

## PG-003 Learn

The Platform SHOULD improve through accumulated Knowledge.

Learning SHALL preserve Household continuity.

---

## PG-004 Adapt

The Platform SHOULD adapt to changes within the Household.

Adaptation SHALL preserve architectural consistency.

---

## PG-005 Explain

The Platform SHOULD explain significant Decisions.

Residents SHOULD understand why Recommendations and Actions occur.

---

# 5.2 Platform Non-Goals

The following objectives are explicitly outside the scope of SmartScreenOS.

SmartScreenOS is NOT:

- an IoT Platform;

- a Device Management System;

- a Dashboard Framework;

- a Home Assistant replacement;

- a Vendor SDK;

- a collection of Device Drivers;

- a protocol gateway.

Device integration supports the Platform.

It does not define the Platform.

---

# 5.3 Household Philosophy

The Household defines the semantic boundary of SmartScreenOS.

The Platform SHALL reason about the Household before reasoning about individual Devices.

The canonical priority is:

Household

↓

Resident

↓

Experience

↓

Capability

↓

Device

Devices exist to serve the Household.

The Household does not exist to serve Devices.

---

# 6. Core Objects

SmartScreenOS models the Household using a small number of fundamental architectural objects.

Every Runtime Component, Capability and Canon Specification SHALL ultimately operate upon these Core Objects.

---

## CO-001 Household

The Household is the highest semantic object.

Every Resident, Room, Device and Experience belongs to exactly one Household.

The Household represents the Platform's complete understanding of the living environment.

---

## CO-002 Entity

An Entity is any uniquely identifiable object represented by the Platform.

Entities include physical, virtual and semantic objects.

Typical Entities include:

- Resident
- Room
- Device
- Capability
- Service
- Zone

Entities SHALL possess stable identities.

---

## CO-003 Observation

Observation is the atomic unit of factual information.

Observations describe what has been directly observed.

Observations SHALL remain immutable after creation.

Observations SHALL NOT contain inferred meaning.

---

## CO-004 State

State represents the latest known condition of an Entity.

State evolves over time as new Observations are received.

Historical Observations SHALL remain preserved independently of current State.

---

## CO-005 Event

An Event represents a meaningful change affecting one or more Entities.

Events are semantic objects rather than hardware notifications.

Multiple Observations MAY contribute to one Event.

One Observation MAY contribute to multiple Events.

---

## CO-006 Context

Context represents all information required to correctly interpret an Observation or Event.

Context MAY include:

- temporal information;
- spatial information;
- environmental information;
- historical information;
- behavioral information;
- relationship information.

Context SHALL remain independent of individual Device implementations.

# 7. Household Foundation

The Household Foundation defines the fundamental domain model of SmartScreenOS.

Unlike traditional automation platforms that organize systems around Devices, SmartScreenOS organizes every architectural object around the Household.

The Household SHALL remain the primary semantic boundary of the Platform.

---

## HF-001 Household As The Root Object

Every architectural object SHALL belong to exactly one Household.

No Device, Resident, Room or Experience SHALL exist independently of a Household.

The Household forms the root of the semantic hierarchy.

---

## HF-002 Household Composition

A Household consists of one or more of the following canonical objects:

- Residents
- Rooms
- Zones
- Devices
- Services
- Capabilities
- Environmental Conditions
- Experiences
- Policies
- Knowledge

Additional object types MAY be introduced by future Canon documents provided they conform to the Household model.

---

## HF-003 Resident-Centric Understanding

Residents represent the primary beneficiaries of the Platform.

The Platform SHALL model Resident behavior, preferences and needs rather than focusing exclusively on Device behavior.

Residents SHALL be represented as semantic entities.

Authentication mechanisms SHALL NOT define Resident identity.

---

## HF-004 Room As Semantic Space

A Room represents both a physical location and a semantic context.

Rooms organize:

- Devices
- Environmental Observations
- Resident Activities
- Experiences

A Room SHALL NOT be treated merely as a container for Devices.

---

## HF-005 Zone As Logical Space

A Zone represents a semantic grouping independent of physical boundaries.

Zones MAY contain:

- one Room;
- multiple Rooms;
- portions of Rooms.

Zones support semantic reasoning rather than physical layout.

Examples include:

- Sleeping Zone
- Entertainment Zone
- Outdoor Zone
- Quiet Zone

---

## HF-006 Household Continuity

The Platform SHALL preserve Household understanding across Runtime restarts.

Household identity SHALL remain stable.

Long-term semantic knowledge SHALL survive transient Runtime failures whenever practical.

---

# 8. Observation Foundation

Observation is the foundational source of all Platform knowledge.

Every semantic process begins with one or more Observations.

No Interpretation SHALL exist without supporting Observations.

---

## OF-001 Observation Is Fact

Observations SHALL represent facts directly obtained from the environment.

Observations SHALL remain free from assumptions, predictions or semantic interpretation.

Examples include:

- Temperature = 25.8°C
- Motion Detected
- Window Open
- CO₂ = 820 ppm

These are factual observations.

They are not conclusions.

---

## OF-002 Observation Immutability

After creation, an Observation SHALL NOT be modified.

If new information becomes available, a new Observation SHALL be generated.

Historical Observations SHALL remain preserved.

---

## OF-003 Observation Identity

Each Observation SHALL possess a unique identity.

Observation identity SHOULD remain stable throughout its lifecycle.

The Platform SHALL be capable of referencing individual Observations during semantic reasoning.

---

## OF-004 Observation Timestamp

Every Observation SHALL include a timestamp.

The timestamp represents the moment at which the Observation occurred or was recorded.

Platform Components SHALL preserve timestamp integrity.

---

## OF-005 Observation Source

Every Observation SHALL identify its originating source.

Possible sources include:

- Device
- Adapter
- Runtime Service
- Capability
- External Platform

The Observation source SHALL remain traceable.

---

## OF-006 Observation Independence

Observation format SHALL remain independent of vendor protocols.

Canonical Observations SHALL be produced before semantic processing begins.

Protocol-specific payloads SHALL remain confined to Adapter implementations.

# 9. Semantic Foundation

Semantic understanding distinguishes SmartScreenOS from traditional automation systems.

The Platform SHALL transform factual information into meaningful understanding through a structured semantic process.

Semantic processing SHALL remain deterministic whenever sufficient information exists and SHALL remain explainable whenever uncertainty is present.

---

## SF-001 Semantic Layer

The Semantic Layer is responsible for assigning meaning to Observations.

The Semantic Layer SHALL operate independently of:

- Device Vendors;
- Communication Protocols;
- Runtime Implementations;
- User Interface Technologies.

The Semantic Layer SHALL provide a stable conceptual model for all higher-level Platform functions.

---

## SF-002 Interpretation

Interpretation is the process of converting Observations into semantic understanding.

Interpretation SHALL consider:

- Context;
- Knowledge;
- Relationships;
- Historical Information;
- Applicable Policies.

Interpretation SHALL NOT directly execute Actions.

---

## SF-003 Inference

Inference derives conclusions from one or more Interpretations.

Every Inference SHALL be supported by available evidence.

When multiple interpretations are possible, the Platform SHOULD preserve confidence information.

Inference SHALL remain traceable to its supporting Observations.

---

## SF-004 Knowledge

Knowledge represents persistent semantic understanding accumulated by the Platform.

Knowledge MAY include:

- Household structure;
- Resident Preferences;
- Device Relationships;
- Environmental Models;
- Behavioral Patterns.

Knowledge SHALL evolve over time while preserving consistency.

---

## SF-005 Context

Context provides the information necessary for correct semantic interpretation.

Context MAY include:

- Time;
- Location;
- Weather;
- Occupancy;
- Historical Activity;
- Environmental Conditions.

The same Observation MAY produce different Interpretations under different Contexts.

---

## SF-006 Semantic Continuity

Semantic understanding SHALL evolve continuously rather than being recreated for every Runtime cycle.

Previously established Knowledge SHOULD contribute to future Interpretations.

Semantic continuity enables long-term understanding of Household behavior.

---

# 10. Runtime Foundation

The Runtime provides the execution environment of SmartScreenOS.

The Runtime is responsible for executing Platform behavior.

The Runtime SHALL NOT define Platform semantics.

Architectural meaning SHALL remain within the Foundation and Semantic Layers.

---

## RF-001 Runtime Responsibility

The Runtime coordinates execution of Platform Components.

Its responsibilities include:

- Component execution;
- State coordination;
- Event processing;
- Service scheduling;
- Resource management.

The Runtime SHALL remain independent from Household semantics.

---

## RF-002 Runtime Independence

The Runtime SHALL remain independent of:

- Device Vendors;
- Communication Protocols;
- Dashboard Applications;
- User Interface Technologies.

Alternative Runtime implementations MAY exist provided they satisfy this Canon.

---

## RF-003 Runtime Reliability

The Runtime SHOULD continue operating despite localized failures.

Component failures SHOULD remain isolated.

Recovery strategies MAY differ between implementations.

This Canon does not prescribe implementation mechanisms.

---

## RF-004 Runtime Observability

The Runtime SHOULD expose sufficient diagnostic information to support:

- Monitoring;
- Validation;
- Troubleshooting;
- Performance Analysis.

Observability SHALL NOT alter Runtime behavior.

---

# 11. Capability Foundation

Capabilities define reusable Platform abilities.

Capabilities describe **what** the Platform can accomplish.

They SHALL NOT define **how** implementation is performed.

---

## CF-001 Capability Definition

A Capability represents one reusable functional ability.

Examples include:

- Thermal Comfort;
- Presence Detection;
- Air Quality Evaluation;
- Activity Recognition;
- Proposal Generation.

---

## CF-002 Capability Independence

Capabilities SHALL remain independent of:

- Device Vendors;
- Runtime Implementations;
- Communication Protocols;
- Dashboard Applications.

Capabilities SHALL be defined by Canonical behavior.

---

## CF-003 Capability Composition

Capabilities MAY depend upon other Capabilities.

Dependencies SHALL remain acyclic.

Capability composition SHALL preserve architectural layering.

---

## CF-004 Capability Contracts

Every Capability SHALL expose one or more Contracts.

Contracts define observable behavior.

Contracts SHALL remain implementation independent.

---

## CF-005 Capability Discoverability

Capabilities SHALL be discoverable by the Platform.

This Canon does not prescribe discovery mechanisms.

Discovery mechanisms SHALL be defined by future Architecture and SDK Canons.

---

# 12. Experience Foundation

Improving Household Experience is the ultimate purpose of SmartScreenOS.

Every architectural decision SHALL be evaluated according to its impact on Household Experience.

Experience is the final outcome of Platform intelligence.

---

## EF-001 Experience First

Household Experience SHALL take precedence over Device optimization.

The Platform SHALL optimize for Resident wellbeing rather than Device utilization.

Examples include:

- improving sleep quality;
- improving thermal comfort;
- reducing unnecessary interruptions;
- simplifying household operation.

---

## EF-002 Human-Centered Decisions

Platform Decisions SHALL benefit Household Residents.

A technically correct Action SHALL NOT be considered successful if it degrades Resident Experience.

---

## EF-003 Explainable Recommendations

Recommendations SHOULD explain:

- why they were generated;
- which Observations contributed;
- which Context influenced the Proposal;
- expected benefits.

Explainability increases Resident trust.

---

## EF-004 Comfort Is Multidimensional

Comfort SHALL be evaluated using multiple semantic dimensions.

Examples include:

- Thermal Comfort;
- Visual Comfort;
- Acoustic Comfort;
- Air Quality Comfort;
- Psychological Comfort.

No single Observation SHALL completely determine Comfort.

---

## EF-005 Learning From Experience

The Platform SHOULD improve future Decisions using accumulated Knowledge.

Learning SHALL preserve:

- Resident Preferences;
- Household Characteristics;
- Successful Outcomes;
- Long-term Patterns.

Learning SHALL NOT violate applicable Privacy Policies.

---

## EF-006 Trust

Resident trust is a foundational requirement of the Platform.

The Platform SHALL behave predictably.

Unexpected automation SHOULD be minimized.

Residents SHOULD remain capable of understanding why significant Decisions were made.

# 13. Design Principles

The following Design Principles govern every architectural decision within SmartScreenOS.

These principles SHALL remain stable throughout the evolution of the Platform.

---

## DP-001 Separation of Concerns

Each architectural Component SHALL have one primary responsibility.

Responsibilities SHALL remain clearly separated.

Business semantics, Runtime execution, presentation and external integration SHALL NOT be unnecessarily coupled.

---

## DP-002 Layer Independence

The Platform SHALL be organized into well-defined architectural layers.

Each layer SHALL depend only upon lower architectural abstractions.

Higher layers SHALL NOT be required by lower layers.

Canonical architectural layers include:

- Foundation
- Semantic
- Runtime
- Capability
- Experience
- Application

---

## DP-003 Canonical Representation

All external information SHALL be transformed into Canonical representations before entering the semantic processing pipeline.

Vendor-specific payloads SHALL remain confined to Adapter implementations.

The Platform SHALL operate exclusively on Canonical models.

---

## DP-004 Loose Coupling

Architectural Components SHOULD minimize dependencies upon one another.

Communication SHOULD occur through published Contracts.

Direct implementation dependencies SHOULD be avoided whenever practical.

---

## DP-005 Extensibility

The Platform SHALL support future expansion without requiring modification of existing foundational principles.

Extensions SHALL integrate through:

- Capabilities;
- Contracts;
- Registries;
- Adapters.

Extensions SHALL remain compliant with Canon requirements.

---

## DP-006 Backward Compatibility

Architectural evolution SHOULD preserve compatibility whenever practical.

Breaking architectural changes SHALL require:

- Canon revision;
- migration guidance;
- version increment.

---

## DP-007 Observability

Platform behavior SHOULD remain observable.

Diagnostic information SHALL support:

- validation;
- troubleshooting;
- architectural analysis;
- operational monitoring.

Observability SHALL NOT alter functional behavior.

---

## DP-008 Testability

Architectural Components SHALL be designed for verification.

Every significant architectural behavior SHOULD be testable through automated or repeatable procedures.

---

# 14. Architectural Constraints

The following constraints SHALL apply to every SmartScreenOS implementation.

---

## AC-001 Vendor Neutrality

The Platform SHALL remain independent of any individual hardware manufacturer, cloud provider or automation ecosystem.

Vendor-specific functionality SHALL be encapsulated by Adapters.

---

## AC-002 Semantic Integrity

Semantic meaning SHALL remain consistent throughout the Platform.

Equivalent concepts SHALL produce equivalent semantic representations.

Semantic ambiguity SHOULD be minimized.

---

## AC-003 Explainable Decisions

Every significant Decision SHOULD be explainable.

The Platform SHALL preserve sufficient reasoning information to support future analysis.

Opaque decision-making SHOULD be avoided.

---

## AC-004 Architectural Consistency

All Platform Components SHALL conform to Canon-defined architectural principles.

Components SHALL NOT introduce conflicting architectural models.

---

## AC-005 Stable Identity

Every long-lived architectural object SHALL possess a stable identity.

Identity SHALL remain independent of transient Runtime state.

Examples include:

- Household
- Resident
- Room
- Device
- Capability

---

## AC-006 Data Integrity

Canonical data SHALL remain internally consistent.

Historical records SHALL NOT be silently rewritten.

Derived information SHALL remain traceable to supporting evidence.

# 15. Compliance

Compliance defines the minimum conformance requirements for every SmartScreenOS implementation.

Every implementation claiming SmartScreenOS compatibility SHALL satisfy the requirements defined by this Canon.

---

## CP-001 Foundation Compliance

Every Platform implementation SHALL comply with the Foundation Principles defined in Section 4.

No implementation SHALL redefine or contradict those principles.

Future Canon documents SHALL extend the Foundation rather than replace it.

---

## CP-002 Canon Compliance

Implementations SHALL comply with all applicable Canon documents.

Compliance SHALL be evaluated against the Canon version declared by the implementation.

Partial compliance SHALL be explicitly documented.

---

## CP-003 Architectural Compliance

Platform Components SHALL conform to the architectural constraints defined by this Canon.

Architecture Reviews SHOULD verify:

- Layer boundaries;
- Dependency rules;
- Canonical terminology;
- Contract consistency;
- Semantic integrity.

---

## CP-004 Runtime Compliance

Runtime implementations SHALL provide:

Runtime implementations SHALL provide an execution environment that conforms to the Runtime Foundation defined by this Canon.

Alternative Runtime implementations MAY exist provided they satisfy the normative requirements of this Canon.

---

## CP-005 Capability Compliance

Capabilities SHALL:

- expose published Contracts;
- preserve Canonical behavior;
- remain implementation independent.

Capability implementations SHOULD remain interchangeable whenever practical.

---

## CP-006 Semantic Compliance

Semantic processing SHALL preserve:

- Observation integrity;
- Context correctness;
- Knowledge consistency;
- Decision traceability.

Semantic implementations SHALL NOT bypass Canon-defined reasoning stages.

---

## CP-007 Experience Compliance

Every significant Platform enhancement SHOULD demonstrate measurable improvement to Household Experience.

Feature completeness alone SHALL NOT constitute successful compliance.

---

## CP-008 Verification

Compliance SHOULD be verified through one or more of the following methods:

- Architecture Review;
- Automated Testing;
- Canon Validation;
- Integration Testing;
- Manual Review.

Verification evidence SHOULD remain traceable.

Decision

The Household SHALL remain the root object of every semantic model.

Rationale

Residents experience the Household rather than individual Devices.

Implications

Decision

Implications

Inference chains SHOULD remain traceable.

Decision
