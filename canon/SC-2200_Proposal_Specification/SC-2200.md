# 1. Purpose

This Canon defines the normative semantic model for Proposals within SmartScreenOS.

A Proposal represents a recommended future course of action derived from semantic understanding.

A Proposal is neither an Action nor a Decision.

Instead, it expresses what the Platform believes SHOULD happen in order to improve Household Experience.

---

# 2. Scope

This Canon applies to every Platform Component that:

- creates Proposals;
- evaluates Proposals;
- ranks Proposals;
- accepts Proposals;
- rejects Proposals;
- converts Proposals into Decisions.

Device execution mechanisms are outside the scope of this Canon.

---

# 3. Proposal Principles

## PP-001 Proposal Is Recommendation

A Proposal SHALL represent a recommendation.

A Proposal SHALL NOT directly change the state of the Household.

---

## PP-002 Proposal Is Explainable

Every Proposal SHALL preserve machine-readable semantic provenance.

Every significant Proposal SHOULD provide a human-readable explanation.

Residents SHOULD be able to understand why significant Proposals exist.

---

## PP-003 Proposal Is Optional

A Proposal MAY be:

- accepted;
- rejected;
- deferred;
- replaced.

Generation of a Proposal SHALL NOT imply execution.

---

## PP-004 Proposal Improves Experience

Every Proposal SHALL ultimately aim to improve Household Experience as defined by SC-1000.

Optimization of Device behavior alone SHALL NOT justify a Proposal.

---

## PP-005 Proposal Is Semantic

Proposals SHALL originate from:

- Knowledge;
- Context;
- Inference;
- Household Goals.

Device Events SHALL NOT generate Proposals directly without semantic interpretation.

---

# 4. Proposal Model

The canonical Proposal generation model is:

```text
Observation
      │
      ▼
Context
      │
      ▼
Interpretation
      │
      ▼
Inference
      │
      ▼
Knowledge
      │
      ▼
Need
      │
      ▼
Proposal
```

A Proposal SHALL address one or more identified Needs.

A Proposal SHALL remain traceable to the Observations, Context, Interpretation, Inference, Knowledge and Needs that produced it.

A Proposal SHALL precede every autonomous Decision.

# 5. Proposal Lifecycle

A Proposal evolves through a well-defined semantic lifecycle.

The lifecycle ensures that every Proposal remains traceable, explainable and controllable.

The canonical lifecycle is:

```text
Generated
      │
      ▼
Validated
      │
      ▼
Evaluated
      │
      ▼
Prioritized
      │
      ▼
Available
      │
      ├── Deferred ──────┐
      │                  │
      ├── Rejected       │
      │                  │
      ├── Replaced       │
      │                  │
      ├── Expired        │
      │                  │
      └── Converted to Decision
                         │
                         ▼
                      Archived
```

Historical Proposal records SHALL remain available for explainability.

---

## PL-001 Proposal Generation

A Proposal SHALL be generated only after semantic reasoning has completed.

Proposal generation MAY consider:

- Context;
- Knowledge;
- Household Goals;
- Resident Preferences;
- Platform Policies.

Proposal generation SHALL NOT originate directly from Device Events.

---

## PL-002 Proposal Validation

Before entering Evaluation, every Proposal SHALL be validated.

Validation SHOULD verify:

- semantic correctness;
- logical consistency;
- policy compliance;
- completeness.

Invalid Proposals SHALL NOT proceed further.

---

## PL-003 Proposal Prioritization

Validated Proposals SHALL be prioritized.

Priority MAY consider:

- urgency;
- household impact;
- resident preference;
- confidence;
- expected experience improvement.

Priority SHALL remain dynamic.

---

## PL-004 Proposal Availability

A validated and prioritized Proposal MAY become available to:

- Residents;
- Decision Engines;
- applicable Platform Policies.

A Proposal MAY be presented through an Application.

Availability or presentation SHALL NOT imply execution.

---

## PL-005 Proposal Resolution

Every Proposal SHALL eventually reach one of the following outcomes:

- Accepted;
- Rejected;
- Deferred;
- Replaced.

The resolution SHALL be recorded.

---

## PL-006 Proposal Archive

Resolved Proposals SHALL be archived.

Archived Proposals SHALL remain immutable.

Archived Proposals SHOULD support:

- explainability;
- auditing;
- learning;
- future optimization.

---

# 6. Proposal Classification

Every Proposal SHALL belong to one primary Proposal Category.

Classification enables consistent semantic processing.

---

## PC-001 Informational Proposal

Provides information to Residents.

Examples include:

- Weather Reminder;
- Energy Report;
- Maintenance Reminder.

Informational Proposals SHALL NOT request autonomous execution.

---

## PC-002 Advisory Proposal

Suggests a preferred future action.

Examples include:

- Open the Window;
- Reduce Air Conditioner Temperature;
- Delay Laundry.

Residents MAY freely accept or reject Advisory Proposals.

---

## PC-003 Optimization Proposal

Suggests improvements to Household Experience.

Examples include:

- Reduce Energy Consumption;
- Improve Sleep Environment;
- Increase Air Quality.

Optimization Proposals SHOULD consider long-term outcomes.

---

## PC-004 Preventive Proposal

Attempts to prevent undesirable future situations.

Examples include:

- Close Window Before Rain;
- Charge Battery Before Peak Pricing;
- Replace Air Filter.

Preventive Proposals SHOULD be generated before risks materialize.

---

## PC-005 Protective Proposal

Addresses safety or security concerns.

Examples include:

- Possible Water Leakage;
- Smoke Detected;
- Door Left Open.

Protective Proposals SHOULD receive elevated priority.

---

## PC-006 Automation Eligibility

Automation Eligibility is an independent attribute rather than a Proposal Category.

A Proposal MAY be classified as:

- Human Review Required;
- Policy Review Required;
- Autonomous Eligible.

Autonomous Eligibility SHALL require:

- sufficient semantic confidence;
- applicable Platform Policies;
- Resident authorization;
- acceptable risk.

Eligibility SHALL NOT guarantee Decision or execution.

# 7. Proposal Evaluation

Proposal Evaluation determines whether a Proposal is suitable for further consideration.

Evaluation SHALL occur before any Proposal is presented to Residents or submitted for autonomous Decision making.

Proposal Evaluation SHALL remain independent of implementation mechanisms.

---

## PE-001 Evaluation Principle

Every Proposal SHALL be evaluated according to:

- Semantic Correctness;
- Context Validity;
- Knowledge Consistency;
- Expected Experience Improvement;
- Confidence.

Only valid Proposals SHOULD proceed.

---

## PE-002 Semantic Correctness

A Proposal SHALL be semantically compatible with:

- Household Context;
- Resident Context;
- Current Activity;
- Platform Knowledge.

Semantically invalid Proposals SHALL be rejected.

---

## PE-003 Context Validity

Every Proposal SHALL reference valid Context.

Expired or invalid Context SHALL invalidate the Proposal unless replaced by equivalent active Context.

---

## PE-004 Knowledge Consistency

Proposal reasoning SHALL remain consistent with Platform Knowledge.

Knowledge conflicts SHALL be resolved before Proposal evaluation continues.

---

## PE-005 Experience Improvement

Every Proposal SHALL estimate its expected contribution to Household Experience.

Experience improvement MAY include:

- Comfort;
- Safety;
- Health;
- Convenience;
- Sustainability;
- Energy Efficiency.

Device optimization alone SHALL NOT satisfy this requirement.

---

## PE-006 Confidence

Proposal Confidence represents the probability that the Proposal will improve Household Experience.

Confidence MAY be:

- High;
- Medium;
- Low;
- Unknown.

Low-confidence Proposals SHOULD require additional supporting Context before progressing.

---

# 8. Proposal Prioritization

Multiple Proposals MAY exist simultaneously.

The Platform SHALL determine consideration and presentation preference through Proposal Prioritization.

---

## PPY-001 Priority Principle

Proposal Priority SHALL reflect expected value to the Household.

Priority SHALL remain dynamic.

Priority MAY change as Context changes.

---

## PPY-002 Priority Factors

Priority evaluation MAY consider:

- Urgency;
- Safety Impact;
- Resident Preference;
- Context Confidence;
- Knowledge Confidence;
- Expected Experience Improvement.

Implementations MAY introduce additional factors.

---

## PPY-003 Household First

When multiple Residents are affected, Household-wide benefit SHOULD take precedence.

Resident-specific preferences SHOULD still be considered whenever practical.

---

## PPY-004 Conflict Handling

Competing Proposals SHALL be compared before presentation.

The Platform SHALL avoid presenting mutually contradictory Proposals whenever possible.

If conflicts remain unresolved, all competing Proposals SHOULD preserve traceability.

---

## PPY-005 Dynamic Reordering

Proposal priorities MAY change whenever:

- Context changes;
- Resident preferences change;
- Knowledge evolves;
- Household goals change.

Priority recalculation SHALL preserve Proposal history.

---

## PPY-006 Priority Explainability

Whenever Proposal priority influences future Decisions, the Platform SHOULD preserve sufficient information to explain:

- why one Proposal ranked above another;
- which Context contributed;
- which Knowledge contributed;
- which evaluation criteria determined the ranking.

# 9. Proposal Resolution

Proposal Resolution determines how a Proposal reaches its final outcome.

Resolution SHALL occur after Proposal Evaluation and Prioritization.

Every Proposal SHALL eventually reach one terminal state.

---

## PR-001 Resolution Principle

A Proposal SHALL reach one of the following outcomes:

- Converted to Decision;
- Rejected;
- Replaced;
- Expired.

A Deferred Proposal remains unresolved and SHALL define conditions for future reevaluation.

Resolution SHALL preserve semantic traceability.

---

## PR-002 Conversion to Decision

A Proposal MAY be converted into a Decision after successful evaluation and authorization.

Conversion MAY follow:

- Resident approval;
- applicable Platform Policy;
- authorized autonomous evaluation.

Conversion to Decision SHALL NOT directly execute an Action.

Decision evaluation and Action execution remain separate stages.

---

## PR-003 Rejection

A Proposal MAY be rejected.

Typical reasons include:

- insufficient Context;
- insufficient Confidence;
- conflicting Knowledge;
- Resident preference;
- Policy restrictions.

Rejected Proposals SHALL remain available for historical analysis.

---

## PR-004 Deferral

A Proposal MAY be deferred.

Deferral indicates that the Proposal remains potentially valid but is not currently appropriate.

Deferred Proposals SHOULD define reevaluation conditions.

Examples include:

- Resident sleeping;
- Household occupied;
- Better environmental conditions expected.

---

## PR-005 Replacement

A Proposal MAY replace another Proposal.

Replacement SHALL preserve:

- Proposal identity;
- semantic relationship;
- historical traceability.

Replacement SHALL NOT erase historical records.

---

## PR-006 Expiration

A Proposal SHALL expire when:

- supporting Context expires;
- Household objectives change;
- Proposal becomes irrelevant.

Expired Proposals SHALL NOT participate in future Decision generation.

---

# 10. Proposal Explainability

Explainability enables Residents and Platform Components to understand why a Proposal exists.

Every significant Proposal SHOULD remain explainable.

---

## PX-001 Explainability Principle

Every Proposal SHALL identify:

- the Need being addressed;
- supporting Context;
- supporting Knowledge;
- semantic provenance;
- expected Household benefit.

Significant Proposals SHOULD additionally provide a human-readable explanation.

---

## PX-002 Traceability

Every Proposal SHALL maintain references to:

- originating Observations;
- contributing Context;
- supporting Knowledge.

The reasoning chain SHALL remain reconstructable.

---

## PX-003 Human-readable Explanation

Implementations SHOULD provide explanations understandable by Residents.

Example:

Instead of:

"Confidence = 0.82"

Prefer:

"Indoor temperature remained above your preferred sleeping range for 45 minutes."

---

## PX-004 Machine-readable Explanation

Proposal explanations SHOULD also support structured processing by Platform Components.

Machine-readable explanations SHALL preserve semantic consistency.

---

## PX-005 Explainability Preservation

Proposal explanations SHALL remain available after Proposal Resolution.

Historical explanations SHALL support:

- auditing;
- debugging;
- learning;
- Resident review.

---

## PX-006 Explainability Integrity

Explanations SHALL accurately reflect the semantic reasoning process.

Implementations SHALL NOT generate explanations inconsistent with the reasoning that produced the Proposal.

---

# 11. Proposal Persistence

Proposal Persistence preserves Proposal history across Runtime sessions.

Persistence supports learning, explainability and long-term Household optimization.

---

## PPS-001 Persistence Principle

Resolved Proposals MAY remain stored beyond Runtime lifetime.

Persistence SHALL preserve:

- Proposal Identity;
- Resolution;
- Context References;
- Knowledge References;
- Confidence;
- Timestamp.

---

## PPS-002 Historical Proposals

Historical Proposals SHALL remain immutable.

Historical records SHALL support:

- semantic analysis;
- trend analysis;
- experience optimization.

---

## PPS-003 Proposal Recovery

Persistent Proposals SHALL be recoverable after Platform restart.

Recovered Proposals SHALL preserve semantic integrity.

---

## PPS-004 Retention

Proposal retention SHALL be governed by Platform Policy.

Retention SHOULD balance:

- explainability;
- privacy;
- storage efficiency;
- learning value.

---

## PPS-005 Learning Support

Historical Proposal data MAY contribute to future semantic learning.

Learning SHALL preserve Canonical semantic definitions.

---

## PPS-006 Privacy

Proposal history SHALL comply with applicable Privacy Policies.

Proposal persistence SHALL NOT expose information beyond authorized Platform Components.

# 12. Proposal Sharing

Proposal Sharing enables multiple Platform Components to access identical semantic recommendations.

Sharing SHALL preserve Proposal identity, semantic consistency and explainability.

Proposal exchange SHALL use Canonical Proposal Objects.

---

## PS-001 Shared Proposal

A Proposal MAY be shared among:

- Semantic Engines;
- Proposal Engines;
- Decision Engines;
- Capabilities;
- Dashboard Applications.

Every consumer SHALL interpret the same Proposal semantics.

---

## PS-002 Proposal Ownership

Every Proposal SHALL identify one owning Platform Component.

Ownership defines responsibility for:

- creation;
- maintenance;
- update;
- retirement.

Ownership MAY change during the Proposal lifecycle.

Ownership changes SHALL remain traceable.

---

## PS-003 Read Consistency

Consumers reading the same Proposal SHALL obtain equivalent semantic meaning.

Implementation differences SHALL NOT alter Proposal semantics.

---

## PS-004 Publication

Only validated Proposals MAY be published.

Invalid Proposals SHALL NOT be distributed.

Proposal updates SHOULD notify interested Platform Components.

---

## PS-005 Access Control

Proposal visibility SHALL comply with Platform Policy.

Different Platform Components MAY possess different access permissions.

Access restrictions SHALL NOT alter Proposal meaning.

---

## PS-006 Canonical Exchange

Every Proposal exchanged within SmartScreenOS SHALL use Canonical Proposal Objects.

Vendor-specific Proposal representations SHALL remain confined to Adapter boundaries.

---

# 13. Compliance

Compliance defines the minimum requirements for every implementation claiming conformance with SC-2200.

---

## CMP-001 Proposal Compliance

Implementations SHALL represent every Proposal using Canonical Proposal Objects.

Proposal semantics SHALL remain independent of implementation technologies.

---

## CMP-002 Semantic Compliance

Every Proposal SHALL originate from semantic reasoning.

Proposal generation SHALL include:

- Observation;
- Context;
- Interpretation;
- Inference;
- Knowledge.

Proposal generation SHALL NOT bypass semantic processing.

---

## CMP-003 Explainability Compliance

Every significant Proposal SHOULD remain explainable.

Proposal history SHALL preserve sufficient information to reconstruct semantic reasoning.

---

## CMP-004 Lifecycle Compliance

Every Proposal SHALL follow the Proposal Lifecycle defined by this Canon.

Lifecycle implementations MAY differ internally.

Canonical semantic behavior SHALL remain identical.

---

## CMP-005 Canon Compliance

Implementations SHALL comply with:

- SC-0000 Canon Governance;
- SC-0100 SmartScreenOS Glossary;
- SC-1000 Foundation Specification;
- SC-2100 Semantic Context Specification.

Future Canon documents SHALL extend Proposal semantics without redefining this Canon.

# Appendix A — Proposal Categories

SmartScreenOS defines the following Proposal Categories.

| Proposal Category | Description |
|-------------------|-------------|
| Informational | Provides information |
| Advisory | Suggests preferred actions |
| Optimization | Improves long-term Household Experience |
| Preventive | Prevents undesirable situations |
| Protective | Improves Household Safety |

Automation Eligibility SHALL be represented separately from the primary Proposal Category.

Every Proposal SHALL belong to one primary Proposal Category.

      │
      ▼
Context
      │
      ▼
Interpretation
      │
      ▼
Inference
      │
      ▼

      │
      ▼

      ▼

      │

      │
