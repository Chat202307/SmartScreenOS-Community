# 1. Purpose

This Canon establishes the official vocabulary of SmartScreenOS.

Every Canon document, source code module, SDK, API, Runtime component, Dashboard application, engineering document and architectural specification SHALL use the terminology defined by this document.

The objectives of this Canon are:

- establish one authoritative vocabulary;
- eliminate ambiguous terminology;
- improve communication across the project;
- provide a stable semantic foundation for future Canon documents;
- ensure long-term architectural consistency.

This document SHALL be regarded as the official dictionary of SmartScreenOS.

---

# 2. Scope

This Canon governs terminology used throughout the SmartScreenOS project.

The scope includes, but is not limited to:

- Governance
- Foundation
- Architecture
- Runtime
- Semantic Layer
- Capability SDK
- Engineering
- Repository
- Dashboard
- Household Model
- Device Model
- User Experience

Implementation details, algorithms and programming techniques are outside the scope of this document.

---

# 3. Authority

SC-0100 is a Normative Canon.

When terminology conflicts occur:

SC-0100 SHALL be considered the authoritative source.

Alternative names MAY appear only as historical references.

Normative Canon documents SHALL reference terminology defined by SC-0100 whenever applicable.

No implementation SHALL redefine Canon terminology.

---

# 4. Glossary Governance

The following principles govern every Canonical Term.

## GG-001 Uniqueness

Each architectural concept SHALL have exactly one preferred Canonical Term.

Synonymous preferred names are prohibited.

---

## GG-002 Stability

Canonical Terms SHOULD remain stable throughout the lifetime of SmartScreenOS.

Renaming an existing Canonical Term SHALL be treated as an architectural change.

---

## GG-003 Precision

Every definition SHALL describe one concept only.

Definitions SHALL avoid compound meanings.

---

## GG-004 Implementation Independence

Canonical Terms SHALL describe architectural concepts rather than implementation techniques.

Definitions SHALL remain independent from:

- programming language;
- framework;
- vendor;
- operating system;
- hardware platform.

---

## GG-005 Traceability

Every Canonical Term SHOULD reference related Canon specifications whenever available.

---

## GG-006 Consistency

The same term SHALL carry the same meaning throughout the entire SmartScreenOS project.

Different meanings SHALL NOT share one Canonical Term.

---

# 18. Canonical Language Principles

The following language principles apply to every Canon document.

---

## CLP-001

One architectural concept SHALL have one preferred Canonical Term.

---

## CLP-002

Canonical Terms SHALL remain implementation independent.

---

## CLP-003

Canonical Terms SHOULD remain stable across Platform versions.

---

## CLP-004

Vendor terminology SHALL NOT replace Canon terminology.

---

## CLP-005

Canon documents SHALL reuse existing Canonical Terms whenever possible.

---

## CLP-006

If a required concept does not exist in SC-0100, the new term SHALL be defined in SC-0100 before being referenced by another Canon.

---

## CLP-007

Normative statements SHALL use RFC 2119 terminology consistently.

---

## CLP-008

Canonical definitions SHALL be concise, precise and testable whenever applicable.

# Appendix A — Canon Writing Rules

The following rules apply to all future Canon documents.

---

## A.1 Canon Vocabulary

Canon documents SHALL use terminology defined by SC-0100.

Alternative wording SHOULD be avoided unless explicitly introduced as historical terminology.

---

## A.2 Definition Structure

Every Canonical Term SHOULD contain the following sections.

- Category
- Definition
- Normative Meaning
- Constraints
- Examples
- Related Terms
- Canon References

---

## A.3 Cross References

Related Terms SHOULD point to existing Canonical Terms.

Canon References SHALL reference the governing Canon whenever available.

---

## A.4 Naming

Canonical Terms SHALL use Title Case.

Examples

- Capability Registry
- Household Model
- Semantic Layer
- Runtime Service

---

## A.5 Future Evolution

New Canonical Terms MAY be added in future versions.

Existing Canonical Terms SHOULD remain backward compatible whenever practical.

Removal of Canonical Terms SHALL follow the lifecycle defined in Section 8.

---

# Appendix B — Glossary Maintenance Policy

SC-0100 is the authoritative vocabulary of SmartScreenOS.

Every future Canon SHALL be reviewed against SC-0100 before publication.

Changes to SC-0100 SHALL be reviewed by the SmartScreenOS Architecture Board.

Normative changes SHALL increment the Canon version according to Semantic Versioning.
