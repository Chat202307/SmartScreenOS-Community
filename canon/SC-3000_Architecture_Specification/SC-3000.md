# 1. Purpose

This Canon defines the normative architecture of SmartScreenOS.

The Architecture specifies how Platform Components cooperate to transform Observations into Household Experience.

This Canon defines:

- architectural layers;
- canonical components;
- semantic processing pipeline;
- service boundaries;
- runtime organization;
- capability organization;
- application boundaries;
- adapter isolation.

Implementation details remain outside the scope of this specification.

---

# 2. Scope

This Canon applies to every Platform implementation.

The specification governs:

- Runtime Architecture;
- Semantic Architecture;
- Capability Architecture;
- Service Architecture;
- Adapter Architecture;
- Application Architecture.

Programming languages, operating systems and deployment environments are implementation concerns and remain outside the scope of this Canon.

---

# 3. Architecture Principles

## AP-001 Semantic First

Semantic meaning SHALL always precede implementation.

Architecture SHALL preserve semantic integrity.

---

## AP-002 Layer Independence

Every architectural layer SHALL remain independent of higher layers.

Lower layers SHALL NOT depend upon higher layers.

---

## AP-003 Canonical Objects

Every Component SHALL exchange Canonical Objects.

Vendor-specific representations SHALL remain isolated inside Adapters.

---

## AP-004 Explainable Processing

Every semantic result SHALL remain explainable.

Architecture SHALL preserve semantic provenance.

---

## AP-005 Component Isolation

Components SHALL communicate through published Contracts.

Components SHALL NOT access each other's internal implementation directly.

---

# 4. Platform Architecture

The canonical SmartScreenOS architecture is:

```text
+------------------------------------------------------+
|                  Applications                        |
+------------------------------------------------------+
|                 Capability Layer                     |
+------------------------------------------------------+
|              Semantic Processing Layer               |
+------------------------------------------------------+
|                Runtime / Services                    |
+------------------------------------------------------+
|                     Adapters                         |
+------------------------------------------------------+
|              Devices / External Systems              |
+------------------------------------------------------+
```

The architecture SHALL preserve one-way dependencies from top to bottom.

Reverse dependencies SHALL NOT exist.

# 5. Canonical Components

SmartScreenOS is composed of a set of Canonical Components.

Each Component owns a well-defined architectural responsibility.

Components SHALL communicate only through published Contracts.

---

## CC-001 Runtime

The Runtime provides the execution environment for the Platform.

Responsibilities include:

- Component scheduling;
- Service coordination;
- Resource management;
- Lifecycle management.

The Runtime SHALL remain independent of semantic processing.

---

## CC-002 Semantic Engine

The Semantic Engine transforms factual Observations into semantic understanding.

Responsibilities include:

- Context construction;
- Interpretation;
- Inference;
- Knowledge generation;
- Need identification.

The Semantic Engine SHALL comply with SC-2100.

---

## CC-003 Proposal Engine

The Proposal Engine generates semantic recommendations.

Responsibilities include:

- Proposal generation;
- Proposal evaluation;
- Proposal prioritization;
- Proposal resolution.

The Proposal Engine SHALL comply with SC-2200.

---

## CC-004 Decision Engine

The Decision Engine evaluates whether a Proposal should become a Decision.

Responsibilities include:

- Policy evaluation;
- Authorization;
- Resident approval;
- Autonomous decision evaluation.

The Decision Engine SHALL NOT generate Proposals directly.

---

## CC-005 Action Engine

The Action Engine executes approved Decisions.

Responsibilities include:

- Action scheduling;
- Action execution;
- Result collection;
- Failure reporting.

The Action Engine SHALL execute only approved Decisions.

---

## CC-006 Knowledge Engine

The Knowledge Engine maintains long-term semantic understanding.

Responsibilities include:

- Knowledge persistence;
- Knowledge evolution;
- Relationship management;
- Semantic learning.
- Need discovery support

Knowledge SHALL remain implementation independent.

---

## CC-007 Capability

Capabilities provide reusable Platform abilities.

Capabilities SHALL consume Canonical Objects.

Capabilities SHALL NOT bypass Semantic processing.

---

## CC-008 Adapter

Adapters isolate external systems.

Responsibilities include:

- Protocol translation;
- Vendor normalization;
- Canonical Object conversion.

Adapters SHALL prevent vendor-specific semantics from entering the Platform.

---

## CC-009 Application

Applications provide user interaction.

Applications MAY:

- display information;
- present Proposals;
- collect Resident input.

Applications SHALL NOT redefine semantic meaning.

---

# 6. Semantic Pipeline

The SmartScreenOS semantic pipeline defines the canonical processing sequence.

Every Platform implementation SHALL preserve this semantic order.

```text
Observation
      │
      ▼
Context
      │
      ▼
Interpretation
      │
      ▼
Inference
      │
      ▼
Knowledge
      │
      ▼
Need
      │
      ▼
Proposal
      │
      ▼
Decision
      │
      ▼
Action
      │
      ▼
Experience
```

---

## SP-001 Observation

Observations provide factual information.

Observations SHALL remain immutable.

---

## SP-002 Context

Context provides semantic circumstances.

Context SHALL support semantic interpretation.

---

## SP-003 Interpretation

Interpretation derives meaning from Observations within Context.

Interpretation SHALL precede Inference.

---

## SP-004 Inference

Inference derives new semantic understanding.

Inference SHALL remain explainable.

---

## SP-005 Knowledge

Knowledge represents accumulated semantic understanding.

Knowledge SHALL support future reasoning.

---

## SP-006 Need

Needs represent semantic gaps between the current Household state and desired Household outcomes.

Needs SHALL originate from Knowledge together with active Context.

Needs SHALL be expressed independently of Device implementations.

---

## SP-007 Proposal

Proposals recommend future actions that address one or more identified Needs.

Proposal generation SHALL comply with SC-2200.

---

## SP-008 Decision

A Decision authorizes whether a Proposal should proceed.

Decision generation SHALL preserve semantic traceability.

---

## SP-009 Action

Actions execute approved Decisions.

Actions SHALL interact with external systems only through Adapters.

---

## SP-010 Experience

Experience represents the final outcome perceived by the Household.

Every architectural component SHALL ultimately contribute to improving Household Experience.

# 7. Runtime Architecture

The Runtime provides the execution foundation of SmartScreenOS.

The Runtime SHALL execute Platform Components without redefining semantic behavior.

Semantic responsibility SHALL remain within the Semantic Layer.

---

## RA-001 Runtime Responsibility

The Runtime SHALL provide:

- Component execution;
- Scheduling;
- Service coordination;
- Resource allocation;
- Lifecycle management.

The Runtime SHALL remain independent of Household semantics.

---

## RA-002 Component Hosting

Every Canonical Component SHALL execute within the Runtime.

The Runtime SHALL manage:

- Semantic Engine;
- Proposal Engine;
- Decision Engine;
- Action Engine;
- Knowledge Engine;
- Capabilities.

Hosting SHALL remain transparent to Component semantics.

---

## RA-003 Event Processing

The Runtime SHALL process Platform Events.

Events MAY originate from:

- Adapters;
- Applications;
- Platform Components;
- Internal Services.

Event ordering SHALL preserve semantic consistency.

---

## RA-004 Scheduling

The Runtime MAY schedule Components independently.

Scheduling SHALL NOT alter Canonical processing order.

Execution optimization SHALL remain implementation specific.

---

## RA-005 Fault Isolation

Failure of one Component SHALL NOT invalidate unrelated Components.

Fault isolation SHOULD minimize Platform disruption.

Recovery mechanisms remain implementation specific.

---

## RA-006 Runtime Observability

The Runtime SHOULD expose:

- execution status;
- resource utilization;
- Component health;
- diagnostic information.

Observability SHALL NOT alter Platform semantics.

---

# 8. Capability Architecture

Capabilities represent reusable semantic abilities.

Capabilities SHALL remain independent from Device implementations.

---

## CA-001 Capability Responsibility

A Capability SHALL provide one reusable semantic function.

Examples include:

- Presence Detection;
- Sleep Analysis;
- Indoor Comfort;
- Air Quality Assessment;
- Energy Optimization.

---

## CA-002 Capability Dependency

Capabilities MAY depend upon:

- Context;
- Knowledge;
- Needs;
- Proposals.

Capabilities SHALL NOT depend directly upon Devices.

Capabilities SHALL NOT depend directly upon Decisions or Actions.

---

## CA-003 Capability Composition

Multiple Capabilities MAY cooperate.

Composition SHALL preserve:

- semantic consistency;
- explainability;
- Component isolation.

Circular Capability dependencies SHALL NOT exist.

---

## CA-004 Capability Contracts

Capabilities SHALL expose published Contracts.

Contracts SHALL define:

- required inputs;
- produced outputs;
- semantic guarantees.

Implementation details SHALL remain hidden.

---

## CA-005 Capability Lifecycle

Capabilities MAY be:

- Installed;
- Enabled;
- Disabled;
- Updated;
- Removed.

Lifecycle operations SHALL preserve Platform integrity.

---

## CA-006 Capability Isolation

Failure of one Capability SHALL NOT invalidate unrelated Capabilities.

Capability failures SHALL remain observable.

Platform semantic integrity SHALL remain preserved.

---

# 9. Service Architecture

Services provide reusable infrastructure supporting Platform Components.

Services SHALL NOT redefine semantic meaning.

---

## SA-001 Service Responsibility

Services MAY provide:

- persistence;
- messaging;
- scheduling;
- authentication;
- logging;
- configuration.

Services SHALL remain implementation independent.

---

## SA-002 Service Contracts

Every Service SHALL expose published Contracts.

Components SHALL communicate with Services exclusively through these Contracts.

---

## SA-003 Service Independence

Services SHALL remain reusable.

Services SHALL NOT embed Capability-specific logic.

---

## SA-004 Service Reliability

Services SHOULD tolerate transient failures.

Temporary Service failure SHALL NOT permanently corrupt semantic information.

---

## SA-005 Service Observability

Services SHOULD expose operational metrics.

Operational metrics SHALL remain separate from semantic information.

---

## SA-006 Service Extensibility

Additional Services MAY be introduced.

Future Services SHALL preserve compatibility with existing Canonical Components.

# 10. Adapter Architecture

Adapters isolate SmartScreenOS from external systems.

Every interaction with Devices, cloud services and third-party platforms SHALL occur through Adapters.

Adapters SHALL translate implementation-specific representations into Canonical Objects.

---

## AA-001 Adapter Responsibility

Adapters SHALL provide:

- Protocol Translation;
- Data Normalization;
- Canonical Object Conversion;
- Error Isolation.

Adapters SHALL NOT perform semantic reasoning.

---

## AA-002 Canonical Boundary

The Adapter Boundary separates implementation-specific information from Platform semantics.

Inside the Platform:

- Observation;
- Context;
- Knowledge;
- Need;
- Proposal;
- Decision.

Outside the Platform:

- MQTT;
- Matter;
- Home Assistant;
- Zigbee;
- BLE;
- REST APIs;
- Vendor SDKs.

Vendor-specific semantics SHALL NOT cross this boundary.

---

## AA-003 Device Adapters

Device Adapters communicate directly with physical Devices.

Examples include:

- Matter Adapter;
- Zigbee Adapter;
- Bluetooth Adapter;
- Modbus Adapter;
- MQTT Adapter.

Device Adapters SHALL produce Canonical Observations.

---

## AA-004 Service Adapters

Service Adapters communicate with external services.

Examples include:

- Weather Services;
- Calendar Services;
- Energy Pricing;
- Cloud Storage.

Service Adapters SHALL normalize external information before publishing it.

---

## AA-005 Adapter Independence

Adapters SHALL remain replaceable.

Replacing one Adapter SHALL NOT affect semantic processing.

---

## AA-006 Adapter Failure

Adapter failures SHALL remain isolated.

Semantic processing SHALL continue whenever sufficient Canonical information remains available.

---

# 11. Application Architecture

Applications provide interaction between Residents and the Platform.

Applications SHALL consume Platform semantics rather than redefine them.

---

## APA-001 Application Responsibility

Applications MAY:

- present information;
- display Context;
- display Proposals;
- collect Resident input;
- visualize Household status.

Applications SHALL remain presentation-oriented.

---

## APA-002 Application Independence

Applications SHALL remain independent from:

- Runtime implementations;
- Device vendors;
- Communication protocols.

Applications SHALL consume Canonical Objects.

---

## APA-003 Resident Interaction

Residents MAY:

- confirm Proposals;
- reject Proposals;
- modify preferences;
- provide semantic feedback.

Applications SHALL forward Resident input using Canonical Objects.

---

## APA-004 Dashboard Applications

Dashboard Applications SHOULD present:

- Household status;
- Context;
- Knowledge;
- Needs;
- Proposals;
- Decisions.

Dashboards SHALL remain passive presentation layers.

---

## APA-005 Multi-Application Support

Multiple Applications MAY coexist.

Different Applications SHALL observe identical semantic meaning.

Presentation differences SHALL NOT alter Platform behavior.

---

## APA-006 Accessibility

Applications SHOULD support multiple interaction methods.

Examples include:

- touch;
- voice;
- mobile;
- wall displays;
- wearable devices.

Accessibility SHALL remain independent from Platform semantics.

---

# 12. Cross-Cutting Concerns

Certain architectural concerns apply to every Platform Component.

These concerns SHALL be preserved throughout the Platform.

---

## CCC-001 Security

Platform Components SHALL protect:

- integrity;
- authenticity;
- confidentiality.

Security mechanisms SHALL remain independent of semantic processing.

---

## CCC-002 Privacy

Resident privacy SHALL be preserved.

Platform Components SHALL minimize unnecessary exposure of personal information.

Privacy policies SHALL apply consistently across the Platform.

---

## CCC-003 Observability

Every Component SHOULD expose operational information suitable for:

- diagnostics;
- monitoring;
- troubleshooting.

Operational information SHALL remain separate from semantic information.

---

## CCC-004 Reliability

Platform Components SHOULD tolerate transient failures.

Failure recovery SHALL preserve semantic consistency.

---

## CCC-005 Scalability

The Platform SHALL support growth in:

- Devices;
- Residents;
- Capabilities;
- Applications;
- External Services.

Scalability SHALL preserve Canonical behavior.

---

## CCC-006 Extensibility

Future Components MAY extend the Platform.

Extensions SHALL comply with:

- SC-1000 Foundation Specification;
- SC-2100 Semantic Context Specification;
- SC-2200 Proposal Specification.

Extensions SHALL NOT redefine existing Canonical semantics.

# 13. Compliance

Compliance defines the minimum architectural requirements for every implementation claiming conformance with SC-3000.

Every Platform implementation SHALL preserve the architectural principles defined by this Canon.

---

## AC-001 Layer Compliance

Implementations SHALL preserve the canonical architectural layers.

Layers SHALL remain:

- semantically independent;
- hierarchically ordered;
- implementation independent.

Reverse dependencies SHALL NOT exist.

---

## AC-002 Component Compliance

Canonical Components SHALL preserve their architectural responsibilities.

Implementations MAY optimize internal behavior.

Component responsibilities SHALL remain unchanged.

---

## AC-003 Semantic Pipeline Compliance

Implementations SHALL preserve the canonical semantic pipeline.

The following order SHALL remain unchanged:

```text
Observation
      │
      ▼
Context
      │
      ▼
Interpretation
      │
      ▼
Inference
      │
      ▼
Knowledge
      │
      ▼

      │
      ▼

      │
      ▼
```

---

All external communication SHALL occur through Adapters.

Canonical Components SHALL remain isolated from:

- communication protocols;
- Device vendors;
- external services.

---
